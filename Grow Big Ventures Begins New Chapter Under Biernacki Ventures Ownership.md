Website Closers, described as the world's largest tech and internet business brokerage, has announced the successful [**acquisition of Grow Big Ventures**](https://revtech-news.com/grow-big-ventures-enters-new-ownership-chapter/) a specialized B2B outbound sales infrastructure company, by Biernacki Ventures Inc. The transaction was facilitated by Paul Vartanian of Website Closers, who managed every aspect of the deal on behalf of the sellers.

A representative of Grow Big Ventures LLC credited Vartanian and the Website Closers team for handling the sale with professionalism, saying the process helped identify a buyer who shares the company's long-term vision going forward.

### **A Proven Platform Built on Infrastructure Ownership**

Since its founding, Grow Big Ventures has partnered with more than 700 B2B organizations across industries including software, healthcare, professional services, agencies, and real estate. Through its proprietary systems and operational expertise, the company has helped clients generate more than $40 million in attributed revenue, establishing itself as a trusted provider of outbound sales infrastructure.

A key differentiator for the firm has been its deep relationships within the email deliverability ecosystem. Grow Big Ventures maintains access to specialized technology providers and infrastructure partners that are generally unavailable to the broader marketplace, giving clients enterprise-level capabilities while improving campaign performance and deliverability. This focus on owning infrastructure, rather than simply outsourcing marketing services, has become the company's defining competitive edge.

### **New Leadership Brings a Corporate and Consulting Background**

The acquisition marks a leadership transition for the company, which was built from the ground up by its founding team into a sales and demand-generation agency serving B2B clients. Stepping in as the new owner is Jay Biernacki, who spent years in the corporate world before moving into marketing consulting, and who now takes on the role of leading Grow Big Ventures forward with a background in marketing strategy and team-building.

### **What the Deal Signals for the Market**

Industry observers note that Grow Big Ventures' acquisition highlights growing demand for companies that deliver predictable revenue through scalable technology and operational excellence  a trend increasingly shaping how buyers evaluate B2B growth and sales infrastructure firms. The deal positions Grow Big Ventures to expand its reach under new ownership while continuing to build on the outbound sales and deliverability infrastructure that established its reputation in the space.
