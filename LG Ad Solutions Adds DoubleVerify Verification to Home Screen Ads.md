LG Ad Solutions has rolled out [**DoubleVerify verification**](https://martech-news.com/lg-ad-solutions-expands-doubleverify-verification/) across its Home Screen advertising inventory, giving advertisers independent protection against fraud and invalid traffic (IVT) on eligible campaigns. The launch is global, so brands can now hold their Home Screen spend to the same verification standards they apply elsewhere.

With this move, LG Ad Solutions becomes the first OEM to offer DoubleVerify verification on Home Screen inventory. The capability covers Menu placements and other IAB standard native ad units.

Until now, Home Screen ads could not be independently verified, which left a gap for advertisers planning connected TV (CTV) campaigns. That barrier is now removed. Brands can include Home Screen placements in their CTV plans and have third-party checks behind them.

The update also supports LG Ad Solutions' broader measurement strategy. Kelly McMahon, EVP of Global Operations at LG Ad Solutions, said advertisers want more than campaign metrics and need confidence in what is working. In her view, measuring outcomes starts with trust in every part of a media investment, and extending independent verification to the Home Screen is a step in that direction.

**Why Verification Matters in CTV Advertising**
-----------------------------------------------

Transparency and accurate measurement are now basic expectations in media buying. Applying them to Home Screen ads answers a clear industry need, especially as this format keeps growing.

Existing DoubleVerify customers can keep the verification approach they already use. That lets them compare Home Screen performance directly against their other media buys and judge campaign quality on consistent terms.

Todd Randak, GM of CTV at DoubleVerify, said advertisers expect the same trusted verification on Home Screen that they get across their other campaigns. He added that bringing DoubleVerify's media quality measurement to this inventory helps brands apply consistent practices across more of their buys.

**What This Means for Advertisers**
-----------------------------------

The expanded verification gives LG Ad Solutions a stronger, more transparent inventory offering. Advertisers can now measure Home Screen campaigns with metrics they already know, and global availability means qualifying advertisers in more markets can use it.
