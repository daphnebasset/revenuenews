The [**Lord & Taylor Zip partnership**](https://revtech-news.com/lord-and-taylor-zip-partnership-boosts-holiday-sales/) was announced on November 24, 2026, as the iconic retailer relaunches and looks to win back shoppers. Zip Co Limited, a digital financial services company known for people-centered payment products, will become a new checkout option for Lord & Taylor. The retailer hopes the move will unlock demand it has not yet reached and add revenue before the holiday season begins.

**A Wider Set of Payment Options**
----------------------------------

Zip will sit alongside the payment methods Lord & Taylor already offers. The retailer believes this will close gaps in its checkout experience and bring in customer groups its current options do not reach. By adding Zip, it aims to turn missed opportunities into completed purchases.

Sina Yenel, Chief Strategy & Brand Officer at Lord & Taylor, said the brand has always focused on meeting customers where they are. As the company begins its next chapter, she explained, offering more ways to pay helps make luxury shopping feel more welcoming and accessible. She added that Zip should help the brand connect with a new generation of shoppers in time for the holidays.

**Why the Payment Strategy Matters**
------------------------------------

The deal points to a broader shift in retail. Offering flexible payments is no longer enough if the payment lineup still leaves out eligible buyers. Lord & Taylor is addressing this by going beyond its existing solutions and engaging shoppers who prefer to pay with Zip.

Peter Volynsky, Chief Commercial Officer at Zip US, made a similar point. He noted that retailers can reach more customers, capture extra demand and convert more browsing into sales by adding Zip. In his view, a smarter payment strategy is what supports sustainable growth.

**Black Friday and Cyber Monday Raise the Stakes**
--------------------------------------------------

With Black Friday only days away, the timing is significant. The partnership prepares Lord & Taylor to get the most out of the holiday rush and lays groundwork for acquiring customers over the longer term. The takeaway is that a strong payment strategy does more than give shoppers choices. It also helps retailers reach more shoppers.

Zip brings considerable scale to the arrangement. In the U.S., it works with more than 32,600 merchant partners and serves 4.7 million active customers. The Lord & Taylor deal further extends Zip's retail presence at a time when demand for flexible payments keeps growing.
