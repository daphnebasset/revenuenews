**Moody's Debuts Zero-Copy Data Sharing to Power Smarter AI Builds**
====================================================================

Moody's Corporation has introduced [**zero-copy data sharing**](https://ittech-news.com/moody-launches-zero-copy-data-sharing-for-ai-platforms/), a new way for customers to tap its connected intelligence directly inside the environments where they build AI agents, models, and analytics. Teams no longer need to move or duplicate data to put it to work.

The offering sits alongside Moody's existing Model Context Protocol (MCP) connectors. Together, they bring Moody's intelligence to the spots where customers already work. Access is available directly from Moody's or through Snowflake Marketplace.

**A Wide View of Companies and Ownership**
------------------------------------------

Moody's maps relationships across more than 650 million public and private entities and tracks over 2 billion ownership links. This gives customers a full picture of how organizations, people, and ownership structures connect. The company's connected intelligence also took the top overall spot in the Chartis RiskTech100® ranking for the fifth year running.

Cristina Pieretti, Head of Digital Content & Innovation at Moody's Analytics, said the launch responds to a simple reality. As AI shapes more high-stakes decisions, the quality, context, and freshness of the underlying intelligence matter more. By placing its decision-grade intelligence directly in customers' build environments, Moody's aims to give those decisions a trustworthy foundation.

**How Zero-Copy Sharing and MCP Connectors Fit Together**
---------------------------------------------------------

The two options serve different needs. Zero-copy data sharing handles large, continuous workloads at scale. MCP connectors let AI agents pull Moody's intelligence on demand, whenever a question or task calls for it.

Through Snowflake Marketplace, customers can access Moody's data inside their own Snowflake accounts as continuously refreshed, ready-to-query tables. These cover:

*   Company firmographics
    
*   Financials
    
*   Ownership structures
    
*   Credit ratings
    
*   Daily probability-of-default measures
    

MCP connectors delivered via Snowflake also give AI agents access to Moody's credit intelligence.

Rodrigo Rocha, Vice President of Global ISV & Enterprise Technology Partnerships at Snowflake, noted that the collaboration makes it easier for customers to bring trusted data into their working environments. With Moody's data and MCP connectors on Snowflake Marketplace, teams can combine Moody's entity and ownership data with their own for credit and counterparty risk analysis, then feed it into models and AI agents.

**A Trusted Context Layer for AI**
----------------------------------

Moody's connected intelligence blends proprietary data, research, analytics, models, and company registry data from more than 200 jurisdictions. Moody's curates, normalizes, and enriches this content, with traceability back to authoritative sources. The result is a trusted context layer that makes AI outputs more explainable and auditable, wherever teams deploy them.
