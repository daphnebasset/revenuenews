Rafay Systems has secured a place on the 2026 [**Deloitte Technology Fast 500™**,](https://revtech-news.com/rafay-ranks-on-2026-deloitte-technology-fast-500-again/) marking its third consecutive year among North America's fastest-growing companies. The infrastructure orchestration and monetization platform for neoclouds, telcos and enterprise AI shared the news today. Now in its 32nd year, the program recognizes fast-growing businesses across technology, media, telecommunications, life sciences, fintech and energy tech. For Rafay, the recognition reflects sustained growth fueled by rising demand from neocloud and sovereign AI cloud providers.

Haseeb Budhani, CEO of Rafay Systems, said operators worldwide are racing to build AI infrastructure, and that acquiring GPUs is the straightforward part. The real challenge, he explained, lies in turning that hardware into a cloud customers can actually purchase from, which is the problem the Rafay Platform is built to solve. He credited the milestone to the leaders and operators who chose to partner with Rafay on their AI journeys.

AI infrastructure demand is no longer limited to the hyperscalers. Neoclouds, telcos and sovereign AI clouds are now building GPU capacity within their own markets, and each one needs software that runs that capacity as a true cloud, complete with self-service access, multi-tenancy, metering and billing. Developing this layer in-house can take years and requires scarce engineering talent, so many operators are turning to a ready-made orchestration layer instead. As AI adoption accelerates, the gap between owning GPUs and running a cloud service has become a defining challenge for new infrastructure providers. Rafay says most of its revenue growth comes from sovereign cloud and neocloud operators building AI Factories.

The Rafay Platform enables operators to deliver bare metal, virtual machines, Kubernetes, SLURM and token-metered model APIs from shared GPU infrastructure. This allows them to sell AI services rather than simply renting hardware by the hour. Rafay Token Factory publishes AI models as token-metered inference services, and the newly added Token Router and Token Budgets capabilities let operators serve frontier and open-source models behind a single endpoint under one spending policy. In practice, operators can launch differentiated, usage-based AI offerings faster without building a full cloud stack from scratch.

Momentum is also visible in the market. In September, Sharon AI signed a five-year agreement to orchestrate up to 150,000 GPUs on Rafay. Beyond the Deloitte ranking, Rafay appeared on the 2026 Inc. 5000 list and won the AI Compute Gamechanger Award at the Fast Mode Awards 2026. These achievements reinforce Rafay's position as a growing name in AI infrastructure orchestration.
