Render, the cloud platform built for application developers, has earned a place on the inaugural 2026 AI 100 list. Newcomer produced the ranking with Bessemer Venture Partners, and the [**Render AI 100 recognition**](https://revtech-news.com/render-named-to-inaugural-2026-ai-100-ranking/) places the company among the world's top 100 private AI companies.

Anurag Goel, founder and CEO of Render, said the team is proud of the honor. Developers chose Render long before AI could write code, he noted, and coding agents now make the same choice for the same reasons. Agents judge infrastructure by success rate, token usage, and time to a working app. Goel said Render gives them the building blocks to ship ambitious software and keep it running, as the company builds a cloud for a world where agents deploy most software.

**How the AI 100 Ranks Top Private AI Companies**
-------------------------------------------------

The AI 100 assessed nominated companies and recognized category leaders using four weighted factors. Market leadership counts most at 35%, followed by estimated valuation at 30%. Operating metrics account for 20%, while people and culture make up the final 15%.

Only independent AI companies qualified, so any business that had gone public or completed a majority strategic or private equity acquisition was excluded.

Honorees span a wide range of categories, including foundation models, AI infrastructure and compute, developer tools and agents, AI apps, vertical AI, security, physical AI and robotics, and deeptech and AI for science.

**What Industry Leaders Are Saying**
------------------------------------

Eric Newcomer, founder of Newcomer, said the AI startup world plays by its own rules, from upstarts with unprecedented revenue growth to ambitious robotics companies. Working with Bessemer, he said, the team rethought what matters in the AI age to spotlight the most promising startups using foundation models to transform the digital and physical worlds.

Byron Deeter, partner at Bessemer Venture Partners, said the list spotlights the 100 companies driving the AI economy, led by founders moving at unprecedented speed and scale. He sees exponential value creation across infrastructure, AI applications, and physical AI, and called this first cohort a starting point and a natural evolution of Bessemer's decade-long Cloud 100 franchise.

Overall, the placement underscores Render's growing role in AI infrastructure as it continues building its cloud for application developers and coding agents.
