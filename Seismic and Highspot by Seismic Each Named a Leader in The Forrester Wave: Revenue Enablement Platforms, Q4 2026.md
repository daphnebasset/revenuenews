**Seismic**, the go-to-market performance company, has announced that both Seismic and Highspot by Seismic have been named Leaders in The Forrester Wave: [Revenue Enablement Platforms](https://revtech-news.com/seismic-named-a-leader-in-revenue-enablement-platforms/), Q4 2026. The announcement came on October 21, 2026. Forrester evaluated the two platforms independently, before Seismic and Highspot completed their merger on August 18, 2026, so each earned its Leader placement on its own.

According to the announcement, Forrester sees the two platforms as suited to different needs. It positions Seismic as the best fit for complex, regulated, global enterprises that want broad enablement capabilities, deep services, and a rigorous link between learning, execution, and revenue outcomes. For Highspot by Seismic, the report calls its vision among the clearest in the market, citing the way it ties enablement activity to execution guidance.

Seismic CEO Rob Tarkoff said the goal in bringing the two companies together was to change what enablement can do, and that the combined strengths help customers move beyond preparing teams toward helping them perform in the moments that win deals. He noted that both platforms earned the recognition independently.

Since the Forrester evaluation, Seismic has kept building toward what it calls agentic revenue execution. At its Seismic Shift 2026 event, the company introduced Deal Agent across both platforms. The agent pulls together CRM activity, meetings, emails, and buyer engagement to flag deal risks, missing stakeholders, and recommended next actions. Seismic says it will continue investing in both platforms while pursuing a shared vision for AI-powered go-to-market performance.

The recognition adds to a strong run of analyst attention. Seismic was previously named a Leader in The Forrester Wave: Revenue Enablement Platforms, Q3 2024, in the 2025 IDC MarketScape for Revenue Enablement Platforms, and in Gartner’s first Magic Quadrant for the category in November 2025. The merger itself, which Seismic announced in February 2026, created what is described as the largest revenue enablement provider by enterprise customer count.
